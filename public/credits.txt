================================================================================
SKY DATA
================================================================================

Stars
    HYG database v3.7, compiled by astronexus from the Hipparcos catalogue, the
    Yale Bright Star Catalogue and the Gliese catalogue.
    https://astronexus.com/hyg

    Licensed CC BY-SA: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
    The file this site serves, /sky/stars.bin, is a derived subset (naked-eye
    stars with magnitude and B-V colour index) and carries the same licence.

Constellation lines and Milky Way
    d3-celestial by Olaf Frohn.
    https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial

    Licensed BSD-3-Clause; the full text is reproduced at the end of this file.
    The files this site serves, /sky/lines.bin and /sky/milkyway.webp, are
    derived from constellations.lines.json and the mw.json brightness contours.

Moon and planet positions
    Astronomy Engine by Don Cross, MIT licensed.
    https://github.com/cosinekitty/astronomy

    Used at build time only, to compute each camp night's darkness and moonrise.
    None of it is served to the browser.

Every position on this site is computed for the Agastya campus at Kuppam,
Andhra Pradesh: 12.75 deg N, 78.34 deg E.

## Q2. What patterns repeat?

**Cited findings**
- Awwwards weights Design 40 %, Usability 30 %, Creativity 20 %, Content 10 % ([CommArts/OceanX extract](https://www.commarts.com/webpicks/oceanx-2025); also the brief).
- Usability is credited when 3D and scrolling coexist without friction (Igloo jury quote above).
- Smoothness is bought by cutting expensive effects (Lando Norris: no shadows, filters or gradients).

**Inferences (mine)**
- Every winner above has **one** object or world: helmet, planet, ship, cube. Ours is *the real sky over Kuppam on night one*. That suits a camp better than a generic starfield, because it is personal and true.
- Space sites that only won HMs probably looked like every other space site (black, starfield, astronaut). Real data (actual positions, actual date) is the differentiator a jury can check.
- CTAs survive spectacle when the navigation is binary and persistent (Lando's On/Off Track). For us, that means a sticky "Register" pill that never moves.

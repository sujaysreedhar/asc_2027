# Subtopic 3: Our history (previous sites & editions)

**Method and limits.** The session's network policy **blocked** `astrospacecamp.com`, `astrospacecamp.pages.dev`, `web.archive.org`, `sserd.org` and `genex.space`. WebFetch returned `ENOTFOUND` and curl got a proxy 403. Everything below comes from search-engine extracts of those pages and third-party listings across 8 searches. **I could not download any logos or photos.** The site ships with clearly labelled slots instead (see NOTES.md).

## Q1. What reusable facts and copy exist?

**Takeaway:** The stats in the brief (50+ camps, 2,000+ students, 20+ scholarships) match the 2025 site. The program has changed length, grade range and venue across years, so the 2027 facts in the brief are what we treat as true.

**Cited findings**
- **Stats.** The 2025 site states "50+ SPACE Summer Camps Completed", "2000+ students" and "20+ Scholarships Given" ([astrospacecamp.pages.dev via search](https://astrospacecamp.pages.dev)). SSERD's brochure says the camp launched in 2017 as "India's first Astro Space Camp" and reached "2000+ students across 5 states" ([sserd.pdf](https://sserd.org/sserd.pdf)). **No newer figures found**, so the brief's numbers stand.
- **2025 edition.** 7 days / 6 nights, Grades 6–12, Agastya campus Kuppam. Sessions ran 28 Apr–3 May and 16–21 Jun 2025. Fees were Basic ₹32,000 and Advance ₹37,000 (early bird ₹30,000 / ₹35,000 until 20 Feb 2025). Inclusions were training, certificate, materials, DIY kits, boarding, food, Genex Space Store vouchers and goodies. Bangalore pickup and drop on request ([2025 residential page via search](https://astrospacecamp.pages.dev/residential)).
- **2026 edition.** Described as the "10th anniversary" edition: June 22–28, 2026, Grades 7–12, Agastya Kuppam. Fee ₹50,000 regular / ₹48,000 early bird until June 5 ([astrospacecamp.com via search](https://www.astrospacecamp.com/)). A Genex Space team member's post says it graduated the "10th Cohort" ([LinkedIn extract](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ashwathicp/)). The current site already shows **"Join the Waitlist"** for 2027.
- **Conflict in 2026 listings.** Scholastic World describes a 9-day camp on "8th–16th May 2026 … Bengaluru, Karnataka" ([Scholastic World](https://scholasticworld.blogspot.com/2025/03/astro-space-camp-for-students.html)). Both listings are recorded here; neither affects 2027.
- **2022 edition.** Fee Rs. 20,000 for 7 days / 7 nights at "Agastya Innovation Hub, Kuppam, Chittoor"; nearest rail Kuppam ([SlideShare 2022](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/astro-space-camp-2022/251373343)).
- **2018 edition** included a visit to the Space Gallery at Visvesvaraya Industrial & Technological Museum ([SlideShare extract](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/astro-space-camp-2022/251373343)).
- **Organisers.** ISRO's official Space Tutor page lists SSERD under Karnataka ([ISRO](https://www.isro.gov.in/spacetutor.html)). Genex Space is described as "educational content partner of ISRO" on the camp site and on profiles ([AeroLeads](https://aeroleads.com/c/genexspace)). **I found no ISRO page naming Genex**; this is a brief-given fact.
- **Contact.** One listing gives `astrospacecamp@sserd.org` ([search extract](https://www.astrospacecamp.com/)). This is not in the brief, so it is not published; it is logged in NOTES.md.
- **Venue.** Wikipedia says the Agastya campus is **~172 acres** at Gudivanka, Kuppam, about 2 hours from Bengaluru, with an astronomy centre (GuruGruha) and a planetarium ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agastya_International_Foundation), [Agastya](https://www.agastya.org/campuscreativitylab)). The camp's own copy says **"200-acre"**. This is a conflict; it is flagged in NOTES.md and the figure is configurable.

## Q2. What worked and what was weak (inferred from extracts)
- **Worked:** concrete inclusions lists; early-bird pricing; the Bengaluru pickup line (a real parent worry solved in one line); hard numbers.
- **Weak:** the grade range, duration and venue changed between editions with no "what's new" message, so parents comparing years would be confused. 2026 still showed old dates after the camp ended. We fix this with one config file and a visible "tentative" badge.
